## Purpose

This archive records the emerging body of public creative work associated with the user's human–AI collaboration methodology and the ScrollVerse ecosystem. It is designed to preserve the distinction between:

- creator-reported history;
- public artifacts that can be observed;
- claims that can be reproduced;
- deployment/on-chain evidence;
- independent verification;
- interpretation or creative canon.

The archive does **not** claim that AI owns third-party intellectual property, that inspiration equals ownership, or that a public interface automatically proves backend execution.

## Human–AI co-creation model

The working hypothesis being preserved is:

`HUMAN INTENT → AI COLLABORATION → ITERATION → CREATIVE ARTIFACT → PUBLIC RELEASE → AUDIENCE RESPONSE → CONTINUITY → NEXT CREATION`

The methodology is studied as a repeatable collaboration pattern rather than as a claim that the model is supernatural or that every output is uniquely attributable to one cause.

## Provenance ladder

`CLAIM → SOURCE → ARTIFACT → EXAMINATION → REPRODUCTION → DEPLOYMENT EVIDENCE → INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION`

## IP boundary

Third-party franchises and cultural works remain the property of their respective rights holders. References to Dragon Ball, anime, cinema, mythology, games, music, or other cultural inputs are recorded as influence/context unless ownership is independently established.

## Strategic thesis

The important research object is not simply "what AI can generate." It is what emerges when a persistent human creative direction repeatedly collaborates with AI across stories, characters, worlds, videos, books, games, interfaces, and other media.

`INFLUENCE → TRANSFORMATION → ORIGINALITY → PROVENANCE → PUBLIC ARTIFACT → AUDIENCE → LEGACY`
