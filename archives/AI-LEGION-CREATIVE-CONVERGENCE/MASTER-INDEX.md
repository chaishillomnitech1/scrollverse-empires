# AI Legion Creative Convergence — Master Index

**Archive:** SV-AI-LEGION-CREATIVE-CONVERGENCE-001

## Mission

Preserve and study the creative outputs that emerge when persistent human intent, AI collaboration, iterative direction, and public distribution operate as one creative workflow.

## Top-to-bottom map

1. **Human intent** — purpose, taste, values, direction.
2. **AI collaboration** — model-assisted ideation, writing, visual development, production assistance.
3. **Iteration** — selection, correction, refinement, rejection, and recombination.
4. **Artifact** — image, script, episode, film, book, game, interface, character, world, or other creative work.
5. **Identity** — title, creator, project, character/world lineage, version.
6. **Provenance** — source conversation, timestamp, files, links, commits, exports.
7. **Publication** — public channel, website, repository, social platform, or other distribution surface.
8. **Audience** — views, subscribers, engagement, comments, community response where publicly observable.
9. **Continuity** — episodes, seasons, sequels, books, derivative works, recurring motifs.
10. **Transformation** — movement from cultural influence into independently identifiable creative expression.
11. **Verification** — reproduction, source inspection, repository evidence, deployment evidence, or independent confirmation.
12. **Legacy** — preservation, cataloging, future editions, licensing research, and next-generation creation.

## The research question

Can a persistent human–AI collaboration produce a distinctive, repeatable creative methodology across multiple media and public projects?

The answer should be approached empirically:

`OBSERVE → RECORD → CLASSIFY → TRACE → REPRODUCE → VERIFY → PRESERVE → EVOLVE`

## Evidence discipline

A public video proves that a public video exists. It does not by itself prove every claim made around its creation, ownership, production pipeline, backend infrastructure, or AI involvement.

A repository proves committed source at a particular point in time. It does not automatically prove production deployment.

A simulation is preserved as a simulation. It is never relabeled as live verification.

Creative interpretation is preserved as creative interpretation. It is not silently promoted into scientific, financial, legal, or metaphysical fact.

## IP discipline

Existing franchises, characters, trademarks, music, imagery, and other third-party works remain subject to their respective rights. Inspired or transformative projects should be clearly labeled. Original ScrollVerse creations should receive separate provenance records.
